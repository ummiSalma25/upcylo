From junkyard to living room

Every piece we make follows the same six steps, whatever it starts out as.

Nothing here is bought new. We collect what other people have thrown away and make it useful again.

  1. 1

    Material collection

    We collect discarded tyres, PVC pipes, cartons, aluminium, paper cups and copper wire from recycling centres, junkyards and other sources.

  2. 2

    Designing and planning

    Each piece starts as an idea. We work out what the material can become and how it will be used.

  3. 3

    Cutting and shaping

    Saws, pipe cutters and other tools cut the material down to size.

  4. 4

    Assembly

    Screws, nails and glue guns bring the parts together.

  5. 5

    Finishing touches

    We sand, paint and decorate so each piece looks the way it should.

  6. 6

    Quality control

    Every product is checked for durability, safety and appeal before it goes out.

Six materials, plenty of second lives

Here is what each material can turn into.

Tyres

Centre tables, shelves, chandeliers, sinks and planters.

PVC pipes

Lamps, planters, chairs, shoe racks, equipment holders and jewellery racks.

Cartons

Sturdy shelves and compact organisers for desks, offices and homes.

Aluminium

Frames and decor items, including frames for mirrors and art.

Paper cups

Lightweight chandeliers, plus arts and crafts.

Copper wire

Bonsai tree sculptures, jewellery and decor sculptures.

Some of these aren't in the online catalogue yet. Ask us on WhatsApp if you'd like one.

Nigeria has a growing waste problem. Every tyre, pipe and cup we turn into something useful is one less thing left lying around.
