We turn waste into things worth keeping

Our story

Nigeria is dealing with more and more waste, and more and more people want eco-friendly products. UPCYLO sits right between the two.

We take what's been thrown away, like tyres, PVC pipes, cartons, aluminium, paper cups and copper wire, and turn it into furniture, lighting and decor for homes, schools, offices, restaurants, places of worship and gardens.

Our mission is to turn waste into artistic, eco-friendly and useful products that support sustainable living, cut environmental impact and bring creativity into everyday spaces.

What we're working towards

Less waste

Reduce waste by upcycling and recycling discarded materials.

Better products

Give customers high quality, unique pieces for different spaces.

A business that lasts

Build a profitable, sustainable business that helps protect the environment.

More awareness

Show people that eco-friendly choices fit into everyday life.
