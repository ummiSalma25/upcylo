Workshops

Learn why recycling and upcycling matter, then get your hands on the materials.

Talk about it, then try it

Our workshops explain why recycling and upcycling matter, then get people making things with real materials like tyres and PVC pipes.

Every session is different, so tell us who's coming and we'll plan around your group.

Understand the problem

We start with why recycling and upcycling matter, and what waste is doing to our surroundings.

Work with real materials

Tyres, PVC pipes and more. You get hands-on instead of just listening.

See what waste can become

We show you finished pieces from our catalogue so you can see what's possible.

Who it's for

We work with groups that want to learn something practical and do some good at the same time.
